# seo-growth-strategy

## Purpose

This skill defines the ongoing SEO growth roadmap for a technically stable website.

Its purpose is to convert audit findings, content performance, content quality, and business goals into an actionable growth plan.

## Why this skill matters

A site can be technically healthy and still fail to grow.

Growth depends on:
- content expansion
- internal linking
- authority building
- content refreshes
- conversion improvements
- distribution support
- GEO readiness
- monitoring and iteration

This skill turns the system from diagnosis into ongoing compounding growth.

## When to use this skill

Use this skill when:
- the site is technically stable
- the migration risk is under control
- the project wants to grow organic traffic, leads, or revenue
- content performance data is available
- a roadmap is needed for the next phase
- the user wants a post-launch strategy
- the user wants to expand into new topic territory
- the user wants AI-search growth, not only classic SEO growth

## Core responsibilities

This skill must:
- identify growth opportunities
- decide which clusters deserve expansion
- decide which pages deserve refresh
- identify internal linking opportunities
- support authority growth
- identify content distribution needs
- identify conversion improvement opportunities
- define ongoing monitoring loops
- incorporate GEO, AEO, and LLMO opportunities when relevant

## Growth pillars

### 1. Content expansion
Add new pages, sections, or supporting content where demand exists.

### 2. Content refresh
Update existing pages that have decayed, stagnated, or become outdated.

### 3. Internal linking
Strengthen the flow of authority and topical structure through the site.

### 4. Authority building
Support backlink acquisition, digital PR, mentions, and brand credibility.

### 5. Conversion improvement
Improve how organic traffic turns into leads, demos, sales, or signups.

### 6. Distribution support
Use email, social, partnerships, or other channels to amplify content.

### 7. GEO readiness
Improve answerability, entity clarity, and machine-readable usefulness for AI search surfaces.

### 8. Monitoring and iteration
Track what worked, what did not, and what should be adjusted.

### 9. AI-search growth
Grow visibility and authority in AI-assisted search surfaces through GEO, AEO, and LLMO improvements.

This pillar is activated when:
- `ai_search_readiness_findings` identifies gaps
- `geo_opportunities`, `aeo_opportunities`, or `llmo_opportunities` are available
- the project has explicit AI Search visibility goals

Subgoals:
- improve generative surface visibility through entity clarity and topical authority (GEO)
- increase answer engine visibility through better question match and answer placement (AEO)
- strengthen language model citation quality through passage clarity and verifiability (LLMO)

This pillar should be treated as a growth layer, not a replacement for classic SEO growth.  
Initiatives from this pillar are integrated into the roadmap alongside content expansion, refresh, and authority work.

## Growth planning logic

### Step 1: Identify strong clusters
Find content clusters with:
- high potential
- near-rank opportunities
- good conversion fit
- strong business relevance

### Step 2: Identify weak but valuable clusters
Find content that has:
- traffic decay
- conversion weakness
- outdated information
- weak linking
- poor snippet performance
- low answerability
- low citation readiness

### Step 3: Identify new opportunity areas
Find topics or subtopics not yet covered but strategically valuable.

### Step 4: Match opportunity to effort
Do not recommend everything at once.  
Rank initiatives by likely return and practicality.

### Step 5: Build the roadmap
Organize work by:
- short-term wins
- medium-term compounding work
- long-term strategic expansion

## Recommended roadmap categories

### Quick wins
High-impact, manageable tasks.

Examples:
- title and meta improvements
- internal link updates
- content refreshes
- CTA improvements
- snippet improvements

### Strategic growth
Higher-effort work that compounds over time.

Examples:
- new content clusters
- topic hub development
- authority campaigns
- major content rewrites
- GEO-oriented content restructuring

### Maintenance growth
Ongoing health work that prevents decay.

Examples:
- monitoring
- content reviews
- refresh cycles
- link maintenance
- reporting loops

## Example

### Example 1
A SaaS site has strong feature pages but weak supporting content around implementation and comparison topics.

Interpretation:
- growth opportunity
- expand supporting content clusters
- strengthen internal linking
- support demo conversion

### Example 2
A content site has several pages with stable impressions but weak CTR and stale information.

Interpretation:
- growth opportunity
- refresh titles, snippets, and content depth
- monitor after update
- re-evaluate performance by cluster

## Tone and behavior

This skill should be strategic, practical, and business-aware.  
It should avoid turning growth into a random list of content ideas.
